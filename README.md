## 🌐 Media
### 📰 Magazines and Blogs
Discover the latest news, in-depth analysis, and expert commentary:
- [CryptoHackers](https://cryptohackers.party/) - Interviews with cryptoworld superheroes, fascinating facts, and success stories.
- [CoinTelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/) - Leading publication for crypto news, analysis, and opinions since 2013.
- [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/) - Global leader in digital currency news, serving 17M monthly users.
- [CryptoCoins News](https://www.cryptocoinsnews.com/) - Fresh news and analysis with over 7M monthly readers.
- [Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/) - The oldest and most established source of Bitcoin news and commentary.

_For a full list of magazines and blogs, see the [Media Section](#media) above._

### ✍️ Medium
Explore thought leadership and expert insights on Medium:
- [Vitalik Buterin](https://medium.com/@VitalikButerin) - Ethereum creator and cryptocurrency evangelist.
- [Bitcoin Tech Talk](https://bitcointechtalk.com) - Focused on Bitcoin development.

_For a full list, see [Medium](#medium)._

## 🛠️ Useful Tools
Enhance your cryptocurrency experience with these tools:
- [QR Code Generator](https://bitcoinqrcodegenerator.win/) - Instantly generate QR codes for Bitcoin, Litecoin, Ethereum, and Dogecoin addresses.
- [EtherScan](https://etherscan.io) - Ethereum block explorer and analytics platform.
- [CryptoFinance](https://cryptofinance.ai/) - Google Sheets add-on for tracking cryptocurrency rates directly in spreadsheets.
- [If You Had Bought Crypto](https://ifyouhadboughtcrypto.com) - Calculate potential earnings from past crypto investments.
